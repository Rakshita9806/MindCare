Self-Help Resources

Stress Management

Take short breaks during study sessions. Try the Pomodoro technique (25 min study, 5 min break).

Breathing Exercises

Inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 4 seconds, exhale for 4 seconds. Repeat 5 times to calm your nervous system.

Mental Wellness

Maintain a regular sleep schedule, stay hydrated, and try to get some sunlight every day.
